Website code hacked together by Tony Hirst in conversation with Claude.ai, inspired by a list of storytelling club dates posted by Peter Giles to the Society for Storytelling Facebook group on Saturday, November 29th, 2025.

All data is manually sourced and manually added to the site, primarily sourced from primary (performer, ticketing, group) websites as well as secondary sources (Facebook groups etc.). Tony Hirst in conversation with Claude.ai.

For corrections, additions, removals, etc., email: montystoryteller AT gmail DOT com / web: montystoryteller.org / fb: montystoryteller / insta: montystoryteller. Tour listings for story shows are for shows that are run several times in reasonably quick succession. I need to think about tour*ing* shows over longer timeframes. Music tours are subject to my personal and opinionated music policy: musicians I like to see, plus I ones I could imagine on a bill that includes a storyteller as support...

Show your appreciation by booking me for a gig, or by buying a little something for yourself: Island Tales; Protest Tales; Dark Tales.
