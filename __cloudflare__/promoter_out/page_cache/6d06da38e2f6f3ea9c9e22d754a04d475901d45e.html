Touching the Light - Storytelling by Sharon Jacksties

Front Room Weston-Super-Mare, Weston-Super-Mare, BS23 1FF
Saturday 28th February 2026
Theatrical award winning storyteller, Sharon Jacksties, returns to Front Room after receiving critical acclaim for her recent piece 'Talking to the Dark'.

Now, as our world turns once more to the light, this new show shares world-wide inspirational stories collected for her most recent book, 'A History of the World in 100 Tales'.

Let their world wide wisdom uplift you as your storyteller unfolds this '...magical, captivating and atmospheric journey...'

Sharon Jacksties is a professional performance storyteller and author, performing and teaching storytelling here and abroad, and is the author of several anthologies celebrating our world heritage of traditional literature.
