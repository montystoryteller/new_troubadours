Welsh Whisperers: Ffion Phillips and the Dragon Storytellers

Come and listen to stories about kings and queens, the Tylwyth Teg and fairies, dragons and monsters and ordinary people as well. Stories of Barmouth, Gwynedd and Wales, stories from long ago and tomorrow. The Dragon Storytellers will be joined by professional storyteller Ffion Phillips.

Ffion is a bilingual storyteller and folk musician with a passion for breathing new enchantment into old tales and reworking scraps of forgotten material into fresh stories. Her work often explores environmentalist themes and the natural world(s), with much of her repertoire rooted deeply in her native Eryri. As part of the 'Harebell Tellers' alongside Ailsa Dixon she has brought her collaborative shows 'Hear us and Hasten' and 5 star sell-out 'Aderyn/Bird' to the Edinburgh Fringe.

Dewch a gwrando ar straeon am freniniaid a brenhinesau, y Tylwyth Teg a tylwyth teg, dreigiau a chreaduriaid ofnadwy a phobl arferol hefyd. Straeon o Bermo, Gwynedd a Chymru, straeon o'r gorffennol a'r dyfodol. Bydd y Dragon Storytellers yn cael eu ymuno gan adroddwr stori proffesiynol Ffion Phillips.

Mae Ffion yn adroddwr stori a cherddor gwerin dwyieithog gyda brwdfrydedd i roi hud newydd i hen straeon ac ailffurfio darnau o ddeunydd anghofiedig yn straeon ffres. Yn aml mae ei gwaith yn archwilio themâu amgylcheddol a'r byd naturiol, gyda llawer o'i repertoire wedi'i wreiddio'n ddwfn yn ei thir frodorol, Eryri. Fel rhan o'r 'Harebell Tellers' ochr yn ochr ag Ailsa Dixon mae hi wedi dod â'i sioeau cydweithredol 'Clywch Ni a Buellwch' a'r 'Aderyn/Bird' a werthodd allan pum sêr i'r Fringe yn Nhrydedd.
