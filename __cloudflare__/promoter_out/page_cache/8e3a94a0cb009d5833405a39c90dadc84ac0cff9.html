Blandford Literary Festival 2026 & Tickets

IMPORTANT INFO: It’s highly unlikely that you will receive an email from us unless it’s in reply to one you’ve sent, or you’re already confirmed as participating. If you get an email from us, please check the address. Ours DOES NOT end in gmail.com. Please check the ‘Contact Us’ tab if necessary.

Here is all the information you need to find out what will be happening in November 2026. Most events will take place between Friday 13th and Sunday 15th November 2026. Regrettably, we’ve had to cancel Elizabeth Cooke’s talk at 4pm and there has been a change to one of the events at 11.30am

GET YOUR TICKETS HERE!

Please see details of the talks and workshops below. Some are free, all others should be £6 per ticket. If you want more information about the talks or authors, visit the author tab or click on an author’s name below. Don’t forget, the Book & Craft Fair on Saturday 14th November 2026 in the Corn Exchange between 10am and 2pm. Most events will take place in Blandford Corn Exchange (this includes the Town Hall and Council Chambers. Others in Blandford Town Museum and Woodhouse Pavilion Gardens. Please check the venues page for more information on these locations.

FRIDAY 13th November 2026

Children’s authors Kate Darbishire and Debs Pharoah Pink will be running closed events at primaries. However, they will also have stalls at the book fair.

Children’s authors Kate Darbishire and Debs Pharoah Pink will be running closed events at primaries. However, they will also have stalls at the book fair.

New Shoes & Old Knickers with May Ellis and Paula Harmon – 2:00-3:00 – Blandford Town Museum £6

May Ellis and Paula Harmon will be speaking about researching their historical novels. From the role of Quakers and secret missions in Somerset, the role of women in shoe factories, via what the well-dressed Edwardian might do with her underwear, May and Paula will share some of the fascinating, and occasionally bizarre, facts they’ve uncovered for their books, including several they’ve had to leave out because no one would believe them.

SATURDAY 14th November 2026

Book & Craft Fair – 10:00 am – 2 pm – Blandford Corn Exchange Main Hall

Champagne in a Broken Teacup with Greg Duncan – 10:00-11:00 – Town Hall (Corn Exchange) £6

Greg Duncan will talk about his novel, inspired by the activities of his aunt who was in the French Resistance and shows how he a blended a fictional story and historical reality. He will also be sharing some of the fascinating things he found such as the law banning the making of croissant in France!

What I learned from Stephen King and How Not to Self-Publish and Audiobook with Jo Chinn – 10:00-11:00 – Council Chambers (Corn Exchange) Free

Family saga novelist Jo Chinn with discuss what she has learned as a writer in conversation with Nat Wood Fox, referring to her book ‘Under the Hickory Tree’ and a fascinating former career as a police superintendent.

Bringing Characters to Life with Carol Cole & Elisabeth Conway – 11:30-12:30 – Town Hall (Corn Exchange) £6

Referring to Carol’s crime mysteries set in the New Forest Murders novels and Elisabeth’s Strands of Gold trilogy set in South East Asia, Carol Cole and Elisabeth Conway will discuss how to bring characters to life in crime and historical fiction.

Keeper of the Flame: Stories of Bone and Hearth with Finola Brennan – 11:30-12:30 – Council Chambers (Corn Exchange) – Free

Step into an immersive storytelling experience that weaves, myth, folklore and faith.
Witness the grand descent of Persephone, Queen of the Underworld; gather in the shadows with the ancestral witches who guard the hidden embers of the land; and emerge into the golden warmth of Saint Bridget.
Guided by a live soundscape of drum, gong and chimes, journeying through time with music. Feel the rhythm in your bones, taste the sweetness on your tongue, smell the magic in the air.
Together we will discover that light and shadow are not enemies but a continuous dance.

One Step Ahead of Reality with Bob Fairbrother & David Orson Newton – 2:15 pm-3:15 pm – Town Hall (Corn Exchange) £6

How do writers turn today’s uncertainties into tomorrow’s page-turners? Join David Orson Newton and Bob Fairbrother as they discuss AI, conflict, technology, geopolitics, and the art of crafting thrillers that feel alarmingly close to reality with former journalist Professor Donald Nordberg. An entertaining and insightful conversation followed by audience Q&A.

Stuart Mysteries and Recipes with Tasting Samples with Georgia Piggott– 2:15 pm- 3:15 pm – Blandford Town Museum £6

Georgia Piggott, author of the historical mysteries The Alice Chronicles, will talking about her books and researching the food of rich and poor from 400 years ago. Georgia will bring samples to taste and recipe cards to try at home.

Let’s be Writers! Let’s Make a Book – Workshop for 6-9 year olds with Jane Skellett – 2:00 pm – 3:30 pm – Woodhouse Gardens Pavilion – Free

Jane Skellett will help you create your own book’ – to take home, with front cover, back cover blurb, etc – – and the content will be your own individual choice of subject. (One parent/guardian per child or family to stay and of course, create a book of their own too!)

Don Nordberg – A career in journalism and ‘The Fleetwood Half-Orphan Asylum’ with Don Nordberg – 4:00 pm-5:00 pm – Town Hall (Corn Exchange) FREE

In a change to the advertised programme, Don Nordberg will be speaking at 4pm at Town Hall in the Corn Exchange about his career in journalism for Reuters and other s news agencies, his work at Bournemouth University and the inspiration for his novel ‘The Fleetwood Half-Orphan Asylum set in the United States.

It’s 1927. After their mother vanishes, two young brothers find themselves facing a six-month stay at an orphanage in the middle of America while their father sorts out his life. Three years on the elder son runs away, leaving the younger behind. The brothers meet again for the first time in more than thirty years at their father’s deathbed. They fight and storm out of the house in different directions. Thirty-odd years further on, the son of the younger brother receives a letter and a notebook, offering revelations about his family’s history. If he’ll come half-way across America …

From Rural Whodunnits to Jurassic Coast Murders? with Heide Goody and Iain Grant, who co-write as Millie Ravensworth – 6:00 pm-7:00 pm – Town Hall (Corn Exchange) £6

Heide Goody and Iain Grant, who co-write as Millie Ravensworth and also collaborate with award-winning Rachel McLean on the Jurassic Coast Mysteries, will be talking about their books, inspiration, Dorset and how co-writing works for them. Heide and Iain will also be running an interactive session for anyone who wishes to join on Sunday 15th at the Museum event.

SUNDAY 15th November 2026

Inspiration & Creation at the Museum with Sylvia Hixson Andrews, Paula Harmon and Nat Wood Fox – 11:00 am- 3:00 pm – Blandford Town Museum Free

Bring your notebook, laptop, sketchbook, paints, camera and join Paula Harmon, Nat Wood Fox and Sylvia Hixson Andrews for a relaxed creative session – be inspired by your surroundings,, take prompts, discuss ideas or simply write, sketch or photograph. Heide and Iain will also be running an interactive storytelling session for anyone who wishes to join. Tours of the museum will be available. A calm day for your inner artist. (Bring your packed lunch too.) Come for part of the time or all of the time.
