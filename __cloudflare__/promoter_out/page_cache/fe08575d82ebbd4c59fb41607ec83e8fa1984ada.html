Stories of the Vikings: creation, destruction, magic - Told by Jason Buck

Presented by: Cafe#9

0 SHEFFIELD: Cafe No9 (info)
P Monday 7th September, 2026
N Door time: 7:00pm, start time: 7:30pm
. See event info or ticket types for age restrictions
C Other

Event information

Jason Buck Storyteller brings you another chance to see his sell-out show of the legends of the Norse, of the Viking Gods, monsters ... and plenty of giants along the way.
From the the creation … of everything ... through adventures with Odin, Loki and Thor, to the death of the bright God Baldur, and Ragnarok … the end of everything

Ages: Adults and families (9+)

"I found Jason through his Viking themed storytelling. It was the best thing the algorithm could have done this year! His events are fun & entertaining, they're engaging & magic." Catalina, Warwickshire

"Amazing Norse tales which have to be heard, so much presence and atmosphere a truly gifted individual." Tanya, Hampshire
