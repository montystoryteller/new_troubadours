    Out of the Blue, Storytelling Fundraiser

    Regular price
     
     

    ‘Out of the Blue’
    Storytelling Night at The Nose
    Friday 24th July, 7pm-10pm

    Join us on Friday 24th July for a special storytelling evening at The Nose. We want to bring more stories to this year’s Walton Arts Festival, and the money raised on this evening will go towards inviting more storytellers.

    Our theme for the night is ‘Out of the Blue’.  Hosted by Cydnei and with stories from Gerry Donlon, Gemma Garwood, Stephen Foster-Pilkington, Katy Bingham- Best, Nancy Stevenson, Lorna MacKinnon, Charlie Rose, Steve Gregory, Mer Richardson, Fay Dingaway & sounds by Catherine Shrubshall.

    A suggested donation is £5, but you can pay what you (more or less), and there will be a prize raffle on the evening. It will be great to see you there!
