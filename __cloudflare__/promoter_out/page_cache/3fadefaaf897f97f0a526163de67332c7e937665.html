Gather by the warmth of the fire in our truly beautiful location. Relax and enjoy music (with maybe a sing along) plus glorious, mystical, magical tales from storyteller Vanessa to light up the winter dark. Hot chocolate and marshmallows will be available. We will be sitting around the fire- but it is outside... so dress in your cosiest layers!
