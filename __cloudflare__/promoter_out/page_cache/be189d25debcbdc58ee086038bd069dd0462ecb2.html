not sure which season ticket to buy?

To help in choosing the best ticket for you, this table compares which Festival events are included with each ticket type. You may also find it useful to read the festival Terms & Conditions which has all the essential information including full explanations of each ticket type.

spread the cost – installments now available

To help spread the cost, we have introduced payment plans. Choose Pay in 3 at the checkout and split your purchase into three monthly interest-free payments.

sidmouth residents discount scheme for event tickets

Discounts will be available to residents who to live within the EX10 postcode area for Pre-Festivals, Ham Lunchtime and Afternoon concerts, Ham Evening concerts, Bulverton evening and Bulverton Late Night Extra events. These are available in person, in advance from Sidmouth Tourist Information Centre (TIC) and the online Box Office, with identifying documents.  Not available on the door.

personal assistant tickets

Personal Assistant Tickets are provided free for those that qualify who require assistance to attend the Festival. For further information including how to apply, please read the relevant section in Terms & Conditions. Please address any accessibility enquiries to access@sidmouthfolkfestival.co.uk.

If you have any access needs please complete our Access Information Form here or email access@sidmouthfolkfestival.co.uk to request a copy in Word.

refunds and exchanges

There are no refunds or exchanges on any tickets and lost tickets cannot be replaced. We advise you to take out appropriate cancellation of holiday insurance that covers the cost of your tickets should you unfortunately need to cancel your attendance at the Festival for any reason.

to book by post or phone

Tickets can also be purchased in person or by phone at Sidmouth Tourist Information Centre. Call 01395 577952 during opening hours and have your credit/debit card ready. All details can be found on the booking form.
