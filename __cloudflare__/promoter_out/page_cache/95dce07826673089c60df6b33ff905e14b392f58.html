Tales Tattled & Told at StonyWords Literary Festival!

Lynette Hill, Stephen Hobbs, Rebecca LaChance, Peter Boyce, 2026 Bard of Stony Stratford, Gill and Patrick Larley

Presented by: Travelingwords Productions
0MILTON KEYNES: The Stables at the Bull Hotel
PSunday 1st February, 2026
N7:00pm

Event information

Breaking News: Yes, we will have the 15th Bard of Stony Stratford joining us on the night. Congratulations to Darren Paul, the joyous Abracadazra himself, on his well-deserved win. Really looking forward to what you bring us!!!

Darren tells us that 'I am British born Jamaican import with mixed ancestry that grew up in London before moving to Milton Keynes at a young age. My Poetry is sometimes existential in its nature with authenticity and truth being used to explore the art of being through the experience of living.

I share as "Abracadazra - The Wizard of Wordplay" the name is a pun based on the ancient Aramaic incantation Abracadabra meaning I create as I speak, do you believe in Magick?'

And Tales Tattled & Told is proud to present Gill and Patrick Larley at StonyWords Literary Festival!

In 'Shapes' storyteller Gill blends a haunting Selkie tale set on the Isle of Harris with an Innuit folk tale, plus a story of the Albrecht Durer painting, 'The Hare.'

Gill is a performance storyteller with a passion for encouraging the imagination to take her audiences to far away places and times! Patrick, a freelance musician and composer, weaves his harp tunes like golden magic around the words of the tales - shapeshifting selkies, hares and bears…

Some of Patrick's recent compositions include his settings of commissioned poems by Shropshire/Welsh poets, A Shropshire Lass, and his Cantata, The Gentle Earth of Wales, based on Welsh folksongs and poetry.

We'll also have the fantastic music of Rebecca LaChance and the joyous stories of Lynette Hill, Pete Boyce and fingers crossed, the artistry of the newly cloaked Bard of Stony Stratford! Hosted by Stephen Hobbs.

This event is intended for ages 16 and older.

Doors open at 7 pm for a 7:30 pm start. Tickets are £12 online through WeGotTickets.com and at the door.

This event takes place in the Stables Bar, the Bull Hotel, High Street, Stony Stratford. Alcoholic and soft drinks will be available from the bar.

Contact Lynette Hill at lynette@talestattledtold.com.
