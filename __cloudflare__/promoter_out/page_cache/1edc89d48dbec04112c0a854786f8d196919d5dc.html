Faery Tale Blues

‍

18TH APRIL 2026

‍

Making a welcome return to St Lawrence Village Hall, folk duo Glenn Koppany and Sue Cain team up once more with traditional storyteller Tony "Monty" Hirst, aka Monty + The AnyWhens, to bring a new set of old stories alongside traditional and original songs inspired by fairy tale themes and the faery folk.

Contemporary folk'n'blues tunes, playful lyrics and perfect harmonies, evocatively combined with stories of the Other Crowd and tales from a time when fairy stories weren't just for children...

The last time they performed at the hall it was a sell-out event. So grab your tickets before it's too late.

Tickets only £8.

‍

BAR OPENS AT 7PM AND WILL STAY OPEN UNTIL LATE

SHOW STARTS APPROX 8PM AND RUNS FOR ABOUT 90 MINUTES, INCLUDING A BREAK HALF-WAY.

Our village hall bar stocks a variety of ales, lagers, ciders, wines and spirits, as well as non-alcoholic & soft drinks. There is also a selection of bar snacks.
