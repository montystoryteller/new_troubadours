the benefits of Fringe TheatreFest membership...

A brochure and two free Fringe Badges (one for you and one to encourage a friend!) through the post. News and updates through the year. The opportunity to help shape the future of Fringe TheatreFest.

details

  • Fringe TheatreFest membership is an excellent way of supporting the event itself and potentially guiding its future.
  • The £10 membership fee includes two free Fringe Badges (one for you and one to encourage a friend!) and a brochure through the post. Membership fees help defray our eco- friendly printing costs - brochures, badges, Frequent-Fringers, posters, leaflets and venue signage.
  • As a member, you receive email updates through the year and invitations to meetings and events.
  • You have the right to take part in the election of the committee that superintends the activities of Fringe TheatreFest and you have the opportunity to contribute to the meetings of that committee.
  • If you would like to join as a member, please submit the form on our members page.
