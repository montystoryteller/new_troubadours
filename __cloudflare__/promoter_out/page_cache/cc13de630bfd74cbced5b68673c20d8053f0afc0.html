Norfolk Folk Tales by The Yarnsmith of Norwich - Dave Tonge

Stories for young and old

Presented by: Ladybelt Country Park Events
0KETTERINGHAM: Village Hall
PSaturday 29th November, 2025
N2:00pm

Event information

The Yarnsmith of Norwich, award winning author and historian Dave Tonge presents an afternoon for young and old. To learn something of this most special county, it's places and people, it's strange and wondrous dialect in a collection of stories.

From dark dangerous dogs and wily wicked worms, to poor penniless pedlers and lanky, long- limbed lads, Norfolk is steeped in stories.

£8 Adult Children £6 ( age 10 yrs +)
