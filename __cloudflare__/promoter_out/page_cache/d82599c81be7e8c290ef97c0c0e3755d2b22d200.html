GuestHouse Storytellers are based in Newhaven.  Artistic Director, Xanthe Gresham Knight is committed to bringing high quality internationally renowned, professional performance storytellers to the local community.  All are welcome. Hillcrest has a great cafe and many of our audience attend regularly. Most nights there is an opportunity to tell a 7 minute story from the floor.  Contact Xanthe if you are interested. For more details go to www. guesthousestorytellers.com
