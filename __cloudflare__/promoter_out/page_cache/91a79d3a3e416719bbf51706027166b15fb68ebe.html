Tales from One Thousand and One Nights

Music & Performance
24 September 2026
19:00 - 21:00

£8.00

Book Now

Event Description

Join storyteller Jessie Wild, who will amaze and astound you with her magical tales

What can save the world from drowning in anger, betrayal, and blood-soaked cycles of retribution? What can stop the slaughter of innocents?

 

For Scheherazade, it was stories.

 

Story after story. Night after night. Until, finally, she pierced the soul of the king.

 

But will there be a happy ending?

 

Join storyteller Jessie Wild, who will amaze and astound you with her magical, and sometimes anarchic, retellings of these classic tales. Marvel at flying wooden horses, a queen who gave birth to a cat, and a case of flatulence fit to fell even the mightiest of egos: a tale not to be sniffed at!

 

Tickets:

Standard £10

Consession (Student / 65+) £8
